HomeFix Supply IT Helpdesk — VPN Setup

Setting up the HomeFix VPN (GlobalProtect) on a company laptop:

1. Open the Company Portal app and install "GlobalProtect VPN" from the Approved list.
2. Launch GlobalProtect and enter the portal address: vpn.homefixsupply.com
3. Sign in with your network credentials and approve the MFA push notification.
4. The status icon turns blue when connected.

Personal devices are not permitted on the VPN. Contractors receive a scoped VPN profile from their sponsoring manager instead.

Common issues:
- "Gateway unreachable" usually means hotel or guest Wi-Fi is blocking the tunnel; switch to a mobile hotspot.
- If the client sits on "Connecting" for more than two minutes, restart the PanGPS service from Task Manager or reboot.
- VPN sessions cap at 12 hours; reconnect after that.
