HomeFix Supply IT Helpdesk — Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

All employees must enroll in MFA within 7 days of onboarding.

Enrollment:
1. Visit mfa.homefixsupply.com and sign in with your network password.
2. Install the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone.
3. Scan the QR code shown on the enrollment page.
4. Approve the test push notification.

Changing phones: enroll the new phone from the same portal BEFORE wiping the old one. If you already wiped the old phone, the service desk can issue a 4-hour bypass code after identity verification (call extension 4357; bypass codes are never sent by email).

Hardware tokens are available for warehouse staff without smartphones; managers request them through the IT catalog.
