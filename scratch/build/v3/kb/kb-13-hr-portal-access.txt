HomeFix Supply IT Helpdesk — HR Portal and Payslip Access

The HR portal (people.homefixsupply.com) uses your network sign-in. Payslips, tax documents, and leave balances live under "My Documents".

Access from personal devices is allowed through the browser with MFA; no VPN required.

Former employees keep payslip access for 24 months at alumni.homefixsupply.com using a personal email registered before departure. The IT service desk cannot see or change payroll data; payroll corrections go through your HR business partner.
