HomeFix Supply IT Helpdesk — Email and Signature Standards

The corporate signature template is applied automatically by the mail gateway; you do not need to configure a local signature. To update the name, title, or phone number shown, edit your profile in the HR portal; changes propagate overnight.

Shared mailboxes: request access through the IT catalog item "Shared Mailbox Access"; the mailbox owner approves, and access appears in Outlook within an hour. Do not share personal credentials to grant mailbox access.

Attachment limit is 35 MB. For larger files use HomeFix Drive links.
