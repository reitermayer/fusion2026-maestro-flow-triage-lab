HomeFix Supply IT Helpdesk — Laptops and Hardware

Laptop refresh happens every 36 months automatically; you will be contacted by IT with shipping instructions.

Battery, keyboard, or screen faults on in-warranty laptops: file a Hardware Repair ticket in the IT catalog; loaner devices ship within 2 business days for repairs longer than 3 days.

Docks and monitors: each employee is entitled to one dock and up to two 27-inch monitors, ordered through the IT catalog with manager approval. Home-office equipment is not collected when you change desks, only when you leave the company.

Spilled liquid or physical damage: report it the same day; accidental damage is covered once per device lifetime.
