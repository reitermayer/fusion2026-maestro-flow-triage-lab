HomeFix Supply IT Helpdesk — Meeting Rooms and AV

Book rooms directly in Outlook; every room calendar is named by site and capacity (e.g., "AUS-3F-Cedar (8)").

Room panels: badge in to start an ad-hoc meeting; the panel shows the next 3 bookings. If the panel is dark, hold the power button under the display for 5 seconds.

Video bars support one-touch join for Teams meetings booked with the room invited. For HDMI-only presenters, use the cable on the table; no drivers are needed on company laptops.

Report AV faults with the QR code on the room panel; facilities triages within 4 business hours.
