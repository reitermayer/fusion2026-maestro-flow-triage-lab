HomeFix Supply IT Helpdesk — Password Reset

How to reset your HomeFix network password:

1. Go to reset.homefixsupply.com from any browser (works off-network).
2. Enter your employee email and click "Send verification code".
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your registered mobile number.
4. Choose a new password: minimum 14 characters, at least one number and one symbol, and it cannot match your last 10 passwords.
5. The new password syncs to email, VPN, and the HR portal within 5 minutes.

If you are locked out after 5 failed attempts, the lockout clears automatically after 15 minutes. If you never registered a mobile number, call the IT service desk at extension 4357 to verify your identity; self-service reset will not work without a registered number.

Passwords expire every 180 days. You will receive email reminders starting 14 days before expiry.
